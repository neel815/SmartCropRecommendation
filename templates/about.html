🌾 About Smart Crop Recommendation

Agriculture is the backbone of many economies, and making the right crop choice can greatly impact a farmer's productivity and income. Our Smart Crop Recommendation System uses machine learning models to analyze soil nutrients, climate conditions, and other environmental factors to suggest the most suitable crop for cultivation.

By leveraging real-time data and advanced algorithms, this platform helps farmers make data-driven decisions, reduce crop failure, and optimize resource use. Whether you're a small-scale farmer or a large agricultural business, our solution can adapt to your needs.

🔬 How It Works
  • Input key soil and weather parameters such as Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, pH, Temperature, Humidity, and Rainfall.
  • Our trained ML model processes the inputs and predicts the ideal crop.
  • Receive clear, actionable crop recommendations instantly.

Empower farmers with modern technology to improve crop yield, ensure food security, and promote sustainable farming practices.
